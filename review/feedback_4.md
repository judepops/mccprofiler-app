Plan is sound and the sequencing is right. Three things.

1. The Aim 3 positive control needs one more check: gene density.

Silenced and bivalent separating at d=−0.73/−0.58 on the amount-corrected substrate is the result the whole SE claim rests on. But your own note says gene_desert remains the largest displacement after amount correction — so genomic context is uncorrected and it's the strongest thing in the space.

Silenced genes are enriched in gene-poor regions. So "silenced genes separate on shape" could still be "silenced genes live in gene deserts." That's the same trap as the amount one, one level over.

The fix is the test you've already validated: stratify by gene density decile and check whether bivalent/silenced separation is retained within strata. You showed archetype separation retains 101.8% under that test, so the machinery exists and you know how to read the output. If separation collapses within strata, the positive control fails and the SE null becomes uninterpretable again.

Run it before writing Aim 3. It's the last thing that could undermine the headline claim.

2. Promote 1.1 above 1.2.

The mechanism-defined housekeeping test does two jobs: it could add a positive finding, and it directly answers the objection that will come from any chromatin biologist ("HK genes form promoter assemblies, why don't they separate?"). If a mechanism-defined set separates where Eisenberg doesn't, that's a much stronger statement about what your features track — annotation vs mechanism — and it upgrades Aim 1.

1.2 recovers variance in components that mostly aren't load-bearing for any current claim. Worth doing, less urgent.

3. Figures aren't scoped.

Phase 2 allocates writing but not figure production, and seven composite figures is substantial work — every one needs the corrected substrate from Phase 0, so they can't start earlier. Worth making it an explicit phase rather than assuming it falls out of writing, otherwise it'll compress against the deadline.

One thing I'd add to Phase 2's "state explicitly" list: that the amount correction was applied after an initial uncorrected analysis, and that the uncorrected version overstated the displacements. Being upfront that the analysis was redone on a corrected basis is stronger than presenting only the final numbers — it's the same reasoning as the self-correction methods paragraph, and an examiner who spots the earlier version in a handoff will respect it.

The Aim 1 honesty — 6 of 9 margins inside one SD, substantial only for LOEUF and immune GWAS — is exactly the right level of candour. Keep it.
